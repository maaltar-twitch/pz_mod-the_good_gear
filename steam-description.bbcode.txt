[h1]The Good Gear (TGG): Armor vs Comfort[/h1]
[i]Fight to live. Dress to fight.[/i]
Build 42 made [b]discomfort[/b] a real trade-off: every vest, helmet and backpack makes your survivor uncomfortable, and an uncomfortable survivor gets stressed. The game shows a moodle and a tiny bar per item. It never shows the total, or what each piece of gear gives you back in protection.

[b]The Good Gear[/b] is a small window that does.

[h2]What it shows[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Your discomfort[/b], in %, with the moodle steps (20, 40, 60, 80).
[*][b]Where it is heading[/b]: two markers on the bar. [b]Yellow[/b]: where your clothing alone takes it. [b]Red[/b]: with your surroundings on top (wet, cold, heat, a bad bed, a corpse, an overloaded vehicle, alcohol). Discomfort climbs slowly up to it, and drops fast when you take things off.
[*]Two foldable sections: [b]clothing discomfort[/b] (your gear, item by item) and [b]environmental and situational discomfort[/b] (what adds to it right now: wet, cold, heat, a bad bed or the floor, dragging a corpse, an overloaded vehicle; alcohol lowers it).
[*][b]Stress and unhappiness[/b], what discomfort leads to: their value in % and the game's moodles on a 4-step scale. The more stressed you are, the worse your aim becomes.
[*][b]Your protection score[/b], out of 100 %: your average bite and scratch protection over the 17 body parts, the same values as the game's Protection tab.
[*][b]Speed penalties[/b] of your gear: movement and attack speed, in %.
[*][b]Every worn item[/b]: its discomfort points, its bite and scratch protection. Items that cause discomfort first, then (optional) the ones that don't. Both sections fold, and the list scrolls when you wear a lot.
[/list]
Find the gear that protects the most for the fewest points.

[h2]How to use it[/h2]
[list]
[*]Press [b]Insert[/b], or right-click a clothing item or a bag and pick [i]The Good Gear (TGG)[/i].
[*]Hover the [b]?[/b] for a plain explanation of the formula.
[*]The small window button next to the cross switches to a [b]discreet panel[/b] in the style of the game's own windows (protection and discomfort as rows of squares, like skill levels). Click it again for the full card.
[*]Drag it anywhere. It reopens where you left it.
[/list]

[h2]Options[/h2]
Options > Mods > The Good Gear:
[list]
[*][b]Key[/b] to open and close the window.
[*][b]Refresh interval[/b], from 1 to 60 seconds (1 s by default). A low value recalculates more often and can slow down a modest PC. Nothing is calculated while the window is closed.
[*][b]Window size[/b]: compact or medium.
[*][b]Game names[/b] for renamed items (on by default).
[*][b]Maximum height[/b], in % of the screen (50 % by default).
[*][b]Show items without discomfort[/b] (on by default).
[*][b]Show the explanation (?)[/b] (on by default).
[*][b]Show environmental discomfort[/b]: red marker and its section (on by default).
[*][b]Green protection values[/b]: bite and scratch values tinted green, darker for more protection (on by default).
[/list]

[h2]Good to know[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Read-only[/b]: it changes nothing in your game and writes no file. Safe to add or remove from an existing save.
[*][b]Client side[/b]: works in single player and multiplayer (it only reads your own character).
[*][b]Tested on Build 42.22 only.[/b] Older 42.x builds are not supported: some values (discomfort first) may be missing there. Every game call is protected, so on another build the window shows less instead of breaking your game. If something looks wrong on your build, tell me which one in the comments.
[*][b]Desensitized[/b] survivors ignore gear discomfort: the window says so.
[*]The formula comes from the game code of 42.22 (it may differ on other builds): discomfort heads for the sum of the worn items, plus 10 per level of Wet, Hypothermia and Hyperthermia, plus 30 to 50 for a bad bed or the floor, 30 for dragging a corpse, 25 per Heavy Load level in a vehicle, times 1 to 0.5 for alcohol, capped at 100.
[/list]

[h2]Français[/h2]
Une petite fenêtre (touche [b]Inser[/b], ou clic droit sur un vêtement) qui montre votre inconfort sur 100, avec deux repères (jaune : jusqu'où vos vêtements seuls le font monter ; rouge : avec l'environnement en plus), votre note de protection sur 100 % (morsures et égratignures sur les 17 zones du corps), les pénalités de vitesse (déplacement, attaque) et, pour chaque objet porté, son inconfort et sa protection. Pour trouver l'équipement qui protège le plus pour le moins d'inconfort. Une vue discrète, dans le style des fenêtres du jeu, s'ouvre avec le bouton à côté de la croix. Lecture seule, côté client, fréquence de mise à jour réglable. Testé seulement sur la build 42.22 : sur une autre build, certaines valeurs peuvent manquer (la fenêtre ne plante pas).
